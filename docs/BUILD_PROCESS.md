# How I built this with AI

1. I started from a real product boundary: AI prepares evidence; a person owns the consequential decision.
2. Codex helped turn that boundary into a synthetic case, an observable demo path, and explicit non-goals before code.
3. The first implementation grew to roughly 4,200 executable lines while protecting hypothetical infrastructure more than the portfolio claim.
4. I stopped it instead of polishing sunk cost.
5. We preserved the product contract and fixtures, discarded the architecture and code, and set a hard 500-line budget.
6. For each material area we checked at most two current primary references, then recorded `practice → decision → omitted`.
7. The rebuild uses one Next.js app, one structured model call, deterministic evidence checks, one human gate, one table, five tests, and five evals.
8. A labeled replay keeps the product inspectable without misrepresenting a live model run.
9. One technical review and at most one corrective pass prevent both AI slop and review-driven scope inflation.
10. I remain responsible for scope, credentials, spend, deployment, and every public claim; commits, tests, eval reports, and the visible demo are the evidence.
